About the Breed

Meet the Mechelse Koekoek.

The Malines — also known as the Mechelse Koekoek — is a rare Belgian heritage breed that has been quietly making its way into Canadian flocks, and for good reason.

Hardy, calm, and strikingly beautiful with their signature coucou (barred) feather pattern, these birds are genuinely exceptional in every category that matters. Developed in the Mechelen region of Belgium, Malines were bred to be dual-purpose birds of the highest quality — and that's exactly what they are.

In Canada, they remain rare — which is part of why we're committed to raising and preserving this breed properly. They deserve to be here, and they deserve to be done right.

The Malines Difference

What Sets Them Apart.

🛡️ Hardy & Resilient

Malines were developed in Belgium and are well-suited to cold, variable climates — making them an excellent choice for Alberta. They thrive outdoors, handle our winters without fuss, and are built for real farm life. No coddling required.

🥚 Excellent Layers

Known for consistent egg production with strong, well-formed shells. Malines hens are reliable layers that continue producing through conditions that slow down lesser breeds. Expect quality eggs from quality birds.

🍗 Superior Meat Quality

Slow-grown Malines are prized for their rich flavour, firm texture, and exceptional meat quality. They're the kind of bird that reminds you what chicken is supposed to taste like. This is not a fast-food breed — and that's exactly the point.

🐔 Calm & Alert

Malines are confident birds with a calm, easy temperament. They're approachable, easy to handle, and integrate well into mixed flocks. A genuine pleasure to work with from day one — an excellent addition to any flock.

Appearance

The Coucou Pattern.

One look at a Malines and you'll understand why people fall for this breed. The distinctive coucou (barred) black-and-white feather pattern is striking in the field and in the flock. These are birds that draw attention — and deserve it.

The pattern is even across the body, giving the bird a bold, graphic appearance unlike most heritage breeds. Combine that with their large, upright frame and calm bearing, and you have a bird that's as impressive to look at as it is to raise.

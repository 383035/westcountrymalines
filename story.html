Who We Are

A Small Farm With a Clear Purpose.

We're a small family farm located near Rocky Mountain House in Central Alberta — and West Country Malines is something we've built with intention from the ground up.

We chose the Malines breed for a reason. They're hardy enough for Alberta winters, calm enough to be a pleasure to work with, and beautiful in a way that turns heads every time. But more than that, they're a breed worth preserving — a rare Belgian heritage bird that deserves to be raised properly, not as a commodity.

Our birds are slow-grown and pasture-raised, with access to fresh air, space to roam, and a natural diet year-round. We don't rush the process. We don't cut corners. Every bird that leaves our farm reflects the care that went into raising it.

We started West Country Malines because we believe that quality starts at the source. If you're looking for purebred Malines chicks, breeding stock, or just want to learn more about this exceptional breed — you've found the right place.

Our Values

How We Do Things.

Every decision we make on this farm comes back to one question: is this the right way to raise these birds? Slow growth means healthier animals with better muscle development, richer flavour, and stronger immune systems. Pasture access means natural behaviour, natural diet, and natural stress levels.

We are near Rocky Mountain House — Central Alberta — which means our birds are raised in real Canadian conditions. They're not greenhouse birds. They're built for this climate and thrive in it.

We keep our numbers intentionally limited. This isn't a volume operation. It's a quality operation. Every bird gets attention. Every hatch gets care. That's not something you can scale away.

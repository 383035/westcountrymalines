Why West Country Malines

Heritage. Quality. Purpose.

That's the West Country way.

Heritage Breed

Malines are a rare Belgian heritage breed known for their hardiness, calm temperament, and beautiful coucou pattern.

Slow-Grown

Our birds are given time to grow naturally for better health, stronger bodies, and superior flavour.

🏡

Pasture-Raised
